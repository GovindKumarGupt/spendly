Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect the information you give us when you register and use Spendly: your name, email address, password, and the expense details you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and notes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to create and secure your account, store and display your expenses, and improve the service. We do not use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in the Spendly database and passwords are stored in hashed form, not as plain text. We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

4. Cookies and Tracking

Spendly uses essential cookies to keep you signed in. We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We may disclose information only if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users and the service.

6. User Rights and Data Deletion

You may view and update your information at any time. You may also ask us to delete your account and all associated expense data.

7. Third-Party Services

Spendly loads web fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when your browser requests them. We do not otherwise share your data with third-party services.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and continued use of Spendly means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact Information

If you have questions about this policy or your data, please contact the Spendly team at support@example.com.
